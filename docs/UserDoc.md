# User Documentation

This guide explains how to use Memora, from account creation to managing and sharing photo albums.

## 1. Registration and Login

To start using Memora, you need a user account.

### Create an Account

Go to the **Register** page and provide:

- A username
- An email address
- A secure password

You can also upload a profile picture, although this step is optional.

### Sign In

If you already have an account, go to the **Login** page and enter your credentials to access your dashboard.

---

## 2. Dashboard

After signing in, you will be redirected to your dashboard, which provides quick access to your albums, invitations, and favorite photos.

### Navigation Bar

The navigation bar gives access to the main features of the application:

#### Home

Return to your dashboard at any time.

#### Search

Search through your albums and public albums shared by other users using keywords and filters.

#### Invitations

Manage album access requests and search for other users to view their profiles or follow them.

#### My Profile

Click your avatar in the top-right corner to:

- Edit your personal information
- Change your profile picture and banner
- View your followers and following list
- Access your published albums
- Manage your account settings

### Dashboard Sections

#### Recent Albums

Displays your most recently created albums, including their cover image and photo count. Click an album to open it directly.

#### Invitations

Provides an overview of your latest album invitations, including the sender and the album concerned.

#### Favorite Photos

Quickly access up to six of your favorite photos.

---

## 3. Album Management

The **My Albums** page contains all albums you own or collaborate on.

### Create an Album

To create a new album:

1. Click the **Create Album** button.
2. Enter a title and description.
3. Upload a cover image.
4. Add tags to help categorize the album.
5. Upload photos and optionally provide:
   - Descriptions
   - Dates
   - Tags

6. Choose the album visibility:
   - **Public**: Visible to everyone.
   - **Private**: Visible only to you.
   - **Restricted**: Accessible only to selected users.

With restricted albums, invited users can receive different permission levels depending on the role you assign to them.

Once completed, save the album to publish it.

### View and Edit an Album

Opening an album gives access to its detailed view.

From this page, you can:

- Upload one or multiple photos
- Edit album information
- Copy the album link to share it
- Add photos to your favorites using the heart icon
- View and post comments on photos

### Comments

Comments allow contributors to discuss and share feedback directly under each photo.

---

## 4. Collaboration and Permissions

### Invite Contributors

Within an album, you can search for users and invite them to collaborate.

### Manage Permissions

Each invited user can be assigned a specific role:

- **Viewer**: Can view the album.
- **Commenter**: Can view and comment on photos.
- **Contributor**: Can view, comment, and add or modify content within the album.

These permissions make it easy to collaborate while maintaining control over your albums.
